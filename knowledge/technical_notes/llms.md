# LLM Engineering — Mohit Naskar

## Hands-on experiments

### GPT-1.5B from Scratch
Trained a GPT-2 XL-scale language model (1.5B parameters) from scratch. Covered architecture decisions, tokenisation, gradient checkpointing and what breaks at that scale.
GitHub: https://github.com/MohitNaskar/GPT1.5BIL

### NanoGPT (10M)
Transformer implemented from scratch in PyTorch. Covers attention mechanisms, positional encodings and training dynamics.
GitHub: https://github.com/MohitNaskar/NANOGPT10M

### LangChain
Practical exploration of LangChain — chains, retrievers, memory, output parsers, production patterns.
GitHub: https://github.com/MohitNaskar/LangchainFundamentals

### LangGraph
Multi-step agentic systems with persistent state across nodes — graph construction, conditional edges, human-in-the-loop checkpoints.
GitHub: https://github.com/MohitNaskar/LangGraph_Fundamentals

### Real-Time Voice Assistant with LiveKit
LiveKit WebRTC transport + STT + LLM inference + TTS pipeline. Focus on latency budget and turn detection.
GitHub: https://github.com/MohitNaskar/VoiceAssistant
